# Privacy Policy for Kapak Olsun (AI Roast Battle)

**Last Updated:** April 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Kapak Olsun**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how our application handles information, specifically regarding the camera and AI processing.

## 2. Information We Collect
### 2.1 Camera and Images
Our app requires access to your camera and photo gallery to perform its core function: generating AI "roasts" or "battles" based on uploaded photos.
*   **Processing:** Images are sent to Google’s Gemini AI API for analysis.
*   **Storage:** We do NOT store your photos on our private servers for longer than the duration of the API request. However, some metadata and the generated text might be stored locally on your device (History) or in our Firebase database for analytics.

### 2.2 Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
We use Firebase to improve app stability and understand usage patterns.
*   **Data collected:** Device model, OS version, app crashes, and basic usage events (e.g., "roast_started").

## 3. How We Use Your Data
*   To provide the AI Roast/Battle service via the Gemini API.
*   To improve app performance via anonymous crash reporting.

## 4. Third-Party Services
*   **Google Gemini AI:** Images are processed by Google's Generative AI.
*   **Firebase:** For analytics, authentication, and error reporting.

## 5. Your Choices
You can revoke camera and gallery permissions at any time through your device settings.
